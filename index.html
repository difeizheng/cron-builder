What is a cron expression?

A cron expression is a compact string of five fields that tells Unix-like systems when to run a job: minute, hour, day-of-month, month, and day-of-week. It powers crontab on Linux servers, CI pipelines, Kubernetes CronJobs, and countless schedulers.

Cheat sheet

ExpressionMeaning
* * * * *Every minute
*/5 * * * *Every 5 minutes
0 * * * *Every hour, on the hour
0 9 * * *Daily at 09:00
0 9 * * 1-5Weekdays at 09:00
30 8 1 * *08:30 on the 1st of every month
0 0 * * 0Midnight every Sunday

Common gotchas

Day-of-month vs weekday: if both are restricted (not *), most cron implementations run the job when either matches — a classic surprise. Timezone: cron runs in the server's timezone; a 9 AM job on a UTC server fires at 5 PM in Shanghai. Missed runs: cron does not catch up on jobs missed during downtime — use anacron or a scheduler with persistence for that.
