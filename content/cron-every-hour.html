0 * * * *

Minute field 0, all other fields * — fires at the top of every hour (00:00, 01:00, 02:00 …), every day.

Gotchas

The midnight pile-up: dozens of jobs scheduled at 0 * * * * all fire at 00:00 together with daily jobs. Offset heavy jobs to odd minutes (7 * * * *) to dodge the stampede.

"Every hour" vs "after reboot": @hourly is equivalent to 0 * * * * but neither runs a missed job after downtime — cron does not catch up.
