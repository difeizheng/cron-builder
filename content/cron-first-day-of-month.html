Cron: Run a Job on the First Day of the Month

Cron on the 1st of the month: 0 9 1 * *. Variants for quarter-end, and the reliable trick for the LAST day of the month (cron can't express it directly).

0 9 1 * *

Minute 0, hour 9, day-of-month 1 — fires at 09:00 on the 1st of every month. The trick: put the day in field 3 and leave day-of-week as *; restrict both and standard cron ORs them instead.

Variants

The last day of the month: cron can't say it directly

Months end on 28, 29, 30 or 31 — no single field value works. The reliable pattern runs on 28–31 and self-checks:

0 9 28-31 * * [ "$(date -d tomorrow +\%d)" = "01" ] && /path/to/month_end_job.sh

Run it at 09:00 on the 28th–31st; the inline test only lets it execute when tomorrow is the 1st. (Escape the % as \% — cron treats raw % as a newline.)
