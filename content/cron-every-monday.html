Cron: Run a Job Every Monday

Cron every Monday: 0 9 * * 1. Weekly variants (midnight, evening, every N weeks) and the monthly-alternative pattern.

0 9 * * 1

Hour 9, minute 0, weekday 1 (Monday), all other fields * — fires every Monday at 09:00.

Weekly variants

GoalExpression
Monday 09:000 9 * * 1
Monday midnight0 0 * * 1
Monday 18:00 (evening report)0 18 * * 1
Monday and Friday 09:000 9 * * 1,5
Every weekday (for contrast)0 9 * * 1-5

"Every other Monday" is not cron's job

Standard cron cannot express "biweekly". Common workarounds: run weekly and check the ISO week number in the job ([ $(( $(date +\%V) % 2 )) -eq 0 ] && your_job), or use a scheduler with interval support.

Prefer month-end reporting?

0 9 28-31 * * runs on the last few days of every month; add a guard in the job ([ "$(date -d tomorrow +\%d)" = "01" ] && your_job) to keep only the true last day.
