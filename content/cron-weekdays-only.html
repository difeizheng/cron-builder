Cron: Run a Job on Weekdays Only

Cron weekdays only: 0 9 * * 1-5. How the weekday field works, why 0 and 7 are both Sunday, and how to skip holidays.

0 9 * * 1-5

The fifth field is day of week: 1-5 means Monday through Friday. This example fires at 09:00 on working days.

Weekday field reference

ValueDay
0 or 7Sunday (both work — a classic surprise)
1–5Monday to Friday
5,6Saturday and Sunday (careful: 5=Fri, 6=Sat)
MON-FRINames work on most modern crons

Variants

GoalExpression
Weekdays at 09:000 9 * * 1-5
Weekdays every 30 min*/30 * * * 1-5
Monday and Thursday only0 9 * * 1,4
First Monday of the monthnot possible in standard cron — needs [ $(date +\%u) -eq 1 ] && your_job style guard

Holidays

Cron has no concept of holidays. Standard practice: run the weekday job and have the job itself exit early if today is in a holiday list file.
