*/15 8-18 * * *

Hour field 8-18 restricts to the 08:00–18:59 window (includes 18:xx runs — use 8-17 to stop at 17:59).

Boundary precision

The hour range is inclusive on both ends: 9-18 includes the 18:00–18:59 slot. If "until 18:00" means the 18:00 run should NOT fire, write 9-17. Off-by-one at the boundary is the most common mistake in window schedules.
