Cron: Run a Job Every 6 Hours

Cron every 6 hours: 0 */6 * * *. See why the start point matters, how to offset (3-59/6), and the 4-runs-a-day math.

0 */6 * * *

Hour field */6 fires at hours 0, 6, 12, 18 — four runs a day, at minute 0.

The offset most people get wrong

*/6 counts from 0, so writing 30 */6 * * * still fires at 00:30, 06:30, 12:30, 18:30 — you cannot shift the hour set with a minute value. To start at 03:00 instead:

GoalExpressionFires at
Every 6 h from midnight0 */6 * * *00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00
Every 6 h offset to 03:000 3-59/6 * * *03:00, 09:00, 15:00, 21:00
Every 6 h at :30 past30 3-59/6 * * *03:30, 09:30, 15:30, 21:30
Four fixed times0 0,6,12,18 * * *Explicit list, clearest to read

Gotchas

Range + step is the offset syntax: 3-59/6 means "start at 3, step 6". */6 always starts at the field minimum.

Server timezone again: a "6-hour heartbeat" is timezone-independent, but which four clock hours it lands on is not — verify with the next-run preview.
