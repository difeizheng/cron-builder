Cron: Run a Job Every 5 Minutes

The cron expression for every 5 minutes is */5 * * * *. See all variants (starting offset, business hours only), gotchas, and generate your own.

*/5 * * * *

*/5 in the minute field means "starting at 0, every 5 minutes" — so the job fires at :00, :05, :10 … :55 of every hour, every day.

Gotchas

Server timezone: cron uses the server's clock. Check with date before assuming 9 AM means your 9 AM.

Overlapping runs: if your job sometimes takes longer than 5 minutes, runs will overlap. Guard with a lockfile or use a scheduler with concurrency control.
