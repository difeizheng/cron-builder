Cron: Run a Job Daily at a Specific Time

How to schedule a cron job daily at any time: 0 9 * * * for 9 AM, 30 8 * * * for 8:30, 0 0 * * * for midnight. Plus timezone traps.

Daily scheduling puts exact values in the minute and hour fields and leaves the rest as *:

Common times

GoalExpression
Every day at 09:000 9 * * *
Every day at 08:3030 8 * * *
Every day at midnight0 0 * * *
Every day at 23:59 (end of day)59 23 * * *
Every day at 14:15 (2:15 PM)15 14 * * *
#1 mistake — swapped fields. The order is minute hour, not hour minute. 30 8 * * * is 08:30, but 8 30 * * * is 30 minutes past 8 o'clock… of hour 30, which never happens and silently never runs.

Timezone trap

Cron fires in the server's local time. A Chinese user scheduling "9 AM Beijing" on a UTC server must write 0 1 * * * (09:00 CST = 01:00 UTC). Verify with date on the server, or set CRON_TZ where supported.
