Cron: Run a Job Every 30 Minutes

Cron every 30 minutes: */30 * * * *. Offset with 15-59/30, stagger multiple jobs, and avoid the double-fire trap at minute 0 and 30.

*/30 * * * *

Fires at :00 and :30 of every hour, every day. The */30 step in the minute field divides the hour into two slots.

Gotchas

Two fields, not one: you cannot write */30 */30 * * * for "every 30 minutes" — the hour step would skip every other hour. Keep hours as *.

Overlap risk doubles: at 30-minute intervals, jobs running longer than 30 minutes collide with themselves. Add a lock or check for a running PID.
