Cron: Run a Job on Weekends Only

Cron weekends only: 0 10 * * 0,6 or 0 10 * * 6-7. Weekend field values, the 0=7=Sunday surprise, and weekday+weekend pairs.

0 10 * * 0,6

Weekday field 0,6 = Sunday and Saturday — 10:00 on both weekend days.

Weekend field reference

GoalExpressionNotes
Both weekend days at 10:000 10 * * 0,6Standard form
Range form0 10 * * 6-7Equivalent — but 7-0 does NOT wrap across the week
Sunday only0 10 * * 0Also 7
Weekend nights at 22:000 22 * * 0,622:00 both days

The two weekend surprises

1. 0 and 7 are both Sunday. 6-7 means Sat-Sun; 5-6 means Fri-Sat. A range like 6-0 does not wrap across the week — it matches nothing on most implementations.

2. Weekend jobs fail differently — nobody is watching. Logging discipline matters twice as much.
